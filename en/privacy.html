Payments are processed exclusively by Apple. ATLAS never has access to your payment methods.

4. Processors

Supabase (database and authentication hosting), Apple (in-app purchases, Sign in with Apple, notifications). No other third parties.

5. Retention

Account data: until the account is deleted. Raw usage statistics: 400 days maximum. After account deletion, remaining statistics are dissociated from any identity.

6. Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, portability, objection and restriction (GDPR). You can delete your account directly in the app (Profile → Settings → Delete account) or write to us.

7. Children

ATLAS is suitable for teenagers and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13 beyond what is described above; creating an account is optional.

8. Security

Encryption in transit (HTTPS), session stored in the iOS Keychain, per-user access rules on every table.
